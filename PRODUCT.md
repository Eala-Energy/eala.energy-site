## Users

The public site must work, in one neutral register, for every audience on eala's messaging matrix at once. No single reader may be assumed:

- **Investors**: seed-round investors now, and infrastructure funds and Gulf sovereign or strategic capital later. They decide whether eala is a serious infrastructure company or "another battery".
- **Energy and data-centre partners**: wind farm operators, grid companies, and AI compute customers. They judge whether the mechanism is technically credible and whether a site or offtake conversation is worth having.
- **Public sector**: Whitehall officials and MPs. They read eala as a contribution to UK and Scottish energy and AI policy.

The visitor's job is to understand what eala does, whether it is credible, and how to start a conversation, using the contact form or info@eala.energy. Audience-specific framing belongs in targeted documents, never on the public site. Examples: sovereign AI and Clean Power 2030 for Whitehall, IRR for funds.

## Product Purpose

eala energy builds distributed AI compute infrastructure at structurally constrained renewable nodes in Scotland. It captures clean power that is curtailed today and converts it into reliable compute, with an embedded energy-resilience layer for operational continuity and grid support. Success for the site means qualified conversations with investors, site and grid partners, compute customers and policymakers.

## Positioning

eala co-locates compute with curtailed wind behind constrained Scottish grid nodes. A proprietary orchestration layer continuously decides whether value is highest in compute, energy resilience, or grid services. It is designed for constrained power, so it does not need the firm grid connection that conventional data centres wait years for. eala is **compute infrastructure, not a battery company**: storage is the energy-resilience layer that serves compute, never the lead noun.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The platform has three parts:
  - containerised AI compute, inference-led and hardware-optional across GPU generations;
  - an energy-resilience layer sized for long curtailment events;
  - a proprietary orchestration layer.
- Approved public figures are **3–10 MW** modular site design, **6–8 hr** on-site energy continuity, and **AI + Grid**. Do not change them without confirmed data.
- Geography: the nodes are **in Scotland**. Legal references and market comparisons stay UK, for example "conventional UK compute sites".
- Terminology:
  - Lead noun: "distributed AI compute infrastructure".
  - Storage is called the "energy-resilience layer" or "energy-continuity layer".
  - Public copy never leads with battery, BESS or storage.
  - Public copy uses no financial jargon: no IRR, arbitrage, LP or returns.
- Register: neutral-institutional, serious and engineering-led. No startup gloss or hype.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with compute and the mechanism, never with storage or with financial returns.
2. Credibility comes from precision: claims that can be sourced, approved figures only, and no invented proof.
3. One neutral register must hold for an investor, an engineer and a civil servant simultaneously.
4. Scotland's curtailed wind is the specific, defensible place eala starts from. Avoid generic "UK grid" framing.
